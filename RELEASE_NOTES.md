Matra now works in every app.
• Type in any text box (Mail, Slack, Chrome, Notes…): Matra checks it when you pause and shows a badge with the number of suggestions.
• Click the badge, or let the panel open by itself, to accept fixes straight into that app.
• Menu-bar icon, ⌃⌥M shortcut, launch at login, and a per-app off switch.
• Google Gemini (free key) or Anthropic Claude: choose in Settings.
After updating, allow Matra in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
