Matra 0.4.2 — Plagiarism check and Citation finder.
• Agents → Plagiarism check: Matra searches the web for your most distinctive sentences and shows how much looks original, with links to any matching pages.
• Agents → Citation finder: Matra finds credible sources for your claims and writes the citation in APA, MLA or Chicago style, ready to copy.
• Both work with Google Gemini (Google Search) and Anthropic Claude (web search). Links open in your browser, and only sites the search actually visited are shown.
