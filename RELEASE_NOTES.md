Matra 0.4.3 — a Grammarly-style counter on every text box.
• When Matra finds something in another app, the dot becomes a pill: a light bulb for improvements (clarity, word choice, tone) and a red number for corrections (spelling, grammar, punctuation).
• Click the red number to go through corrections one by one, or the bulb for improvements. Each card says "3 corrections" or "2 improvements", and fixes go straight into the app.
• Hover the pill for quick actions (turn off in this app, Write with AI). You can still drag it anywhere.
