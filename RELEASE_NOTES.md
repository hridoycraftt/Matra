Matra 0.4.0
• Google Gemini works in the Mac app: the app's security rules now allow Google's server (it showed "Can't reach the AI service" before, even when you were online).
• A short welcome guide on first launch: connect an AI, allow Matra in other apps, try a check. Every step can be skipped.
• Underline style for color-blind users: Settings → General → "Color and pattern" gives each category its own line (solid, dashed, dotted, double), in Matra and in other apps.
• Snippets: type a trigger like ";sig" and a space to insert saved text (Settings → Snippets).
• Agents tab: Reader reactions, AI grader and an AI-writing estimate.
• New icons (Hugeicons) and Settings with section icons.
• Signed with Matra's own certificate: allow Accessibility once more after this update; later updates keep the permission.
